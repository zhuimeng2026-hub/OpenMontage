# Video Template Remix — Executive Producer

## When to Use

Use this orchestrator for reference-faithful remixes where the original timeline is the template and only approved material may change.

## Prerequisites
Read the manifest, source-media review/video analysis, and reviewer/checkpoint skills. Confirm the source is authorized and identify the exact replacement brief before any generation.

## Process
Run idea → script → scene_plan → assets → edit → compose → publish serially. Keep a ledger of each source scene and its `preserve`, `replace`, or `delete` decision. Use the cheapest deterministic/local tool first; paid generation is opt-in and never a silent fallback. Send a stage back when timing, rights, or slot policy is incomplete.

## Self-Evaluate
- 0–2: source timeline is incomplete or policy is ambiguous.
- 3–4: complete timeline and provenance, but one quality warning remains.
- 5: every scene auditable; only approved slots changed; duration, audio, subtitles, and transitions verified.

## Pitfalls
Do not treat “similar” as permission to copy. Do not infer replace slots from aesthetics. Never hide a failed analyzer behind a successful-looking artifact.
