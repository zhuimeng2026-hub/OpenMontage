# Video Template Remix — Publish Director

## When to Use

Use this final stage after render and review artifacts prove that only approved slots changed.

## Prerequisites
Read render report, final review, brief, and provenance ledger.

## Process
Package the master and explicitly labeled derivatives. Include source attribution/permission notes, replacement asset provenance, preserved/replaced/deleted counts, duration and codec QA, and known limitations. Never imply the remix is an original source recording.

## Self-Evaluate
5 means the export is reproducible and rights/audit metadata travels with it; 3 means a non-blocking metadata omission; 0 means missing output or misleading attribution.

## Pitfalls
Do not strip provenance from copied source elements. Do not publish a degraded render as final without a visible warning.
