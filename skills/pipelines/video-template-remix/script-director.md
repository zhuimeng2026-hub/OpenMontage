# Video Template Remix — Script Director

## When to Use

Use this stage when the remix needs a source-grounded transcript or timed text plan.

## Prerequisites
Read `brief` and any source transcript/analysis. Use transcription only to recover source speech; do not invent narration.

## Process
Align transcript sections to source time ranges. Preserve wording and speech duration by default. Mark subtitle text/positions as preserved unless an approved replacement changes on-screen copy. If transcription fails, report the gap and use timecoded silent beats rather than guessing.

## Self-Evaluate
5 means every spoken or title beat maps to a source range and has a duration-safe treatment; 3 means minor uncertain words; 0 means invented or untimecoded copy.

## Pitfalls
Do not stretch speech to fit generated visuals. Do not silently translate, dub, or remove source audio.
